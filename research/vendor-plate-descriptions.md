## Surface descriptions (verbatim from owner)

**Garolite** — This 1/32" thick topper is primarily used for Nylon. It offers the best
adhesion and reusability. Use your build plate again and again to print any type of nylon
and nylon blends such as glass fiber and carbon filled. It can also be lightly sanded to
yield even greater adhesion. We recommend using this with Elmer's Purple Glue Stick. If
you print with nylon a lot, then you absolutely need this.

**G10 Garolite** — A 1/64" thick good all-around surface for most engineering polymers
including PA, PC, PETG, PCTG, ABS, ASA, TPU, and PLA. Although it is primarily known for
PETG/PCTG as being the best smooth cool-to-release surface on the market. We use true
industrial grade G10 - this is not the same as the "G10" surface from Darkmoon. It has a
glossier finish unlike the standard Garolite option above. It is the most wear resistant
build plate surface we offer. It is like glass as far as durability. You can print 1000's
of prints overtop the same area without degradation. We recommend using this with Elmer's
Purple Glue Stick if printing difficult Nylon parts (may not need glue at all). No
adhesion glue is needed with PETG/PCTG. If you print with PETG/PCTG a lot, then you
absolutely need this. Works with ASA but will require Vision Miner's Nano Polymer
Adhesive and will self-release clean once cool.

**G11 Garolite** — This is also a 1/32" thick topper and can be used for Nylon 12,
Carbon Fiber Nylon, and TPU. Although it is primarily chosen for TPU. It has a more
glossy finish unlike the standard Garolite option. It is the most wear resistant build
plate surface we offer. It is like glass as far as durability. You can print 1000's of
prints overtop the same area without degradation. No adhesion glue is needed with
Nylon 12 or TPU. If you print with TPU a lot, then you absolutely need this.

**Rigid G10 Garolite** — Standard 1/64" G10 laminated to a thick steel plate. The plate
is rigid and will not flex. Best suited for high warp parts that can curl a spring steel
plate like a potato chip. Depending on the material; parts can be removed with little
effort once the plate has cooled to room temperature. Works with ASA but will require
Vision Miner's Nano Polymer Adhesive and will self release clean once cool.

**Rigid G11 Garolite** — Standard 1/32" G11 laminated to a thick steel plate. The plate
is rigid and will not flex. Best suited for high warp parts that can curl a spring steel
plate like a potato chip.

**GeckoTek EZ-Stick Hot** — This topper is good for a lot of different materials. We have
found it to hold an extreme grip with Nylon and PETG but the coating wears off after a
few prints in that area. Nylon wears the coating off faster than PETG. This is our go to
for a one off print of a very high warping Nylon print. PETG prints also adhere really
well but will also degrade the coating on the topper after a dozen prints or so. PLA it
works very well for giving a glass like bottom without degrading the topper's coating.
We recommend this if you're printing PLA. No adhesive glues are to be used with this at
all regardless of the material. Please see GeckoTek's website for processing temperatures.

**BuildTak** — This is a good all around topper that can be used plain without adhesion
glue or with your choice of adhesion glue for larger or higher warp prints.

**Polypropylene** — Works well for printing PP with no adhesion glue. Has a very strong
hold! High warping prints will pull the build plate up before it becomes detached from
the surface. Therefore glue should be used on parts that have a large first layer. Large
surface areas can become hard to remove and therefore the glue acts like a release agent.
Magigoo PP is a good product to use with this surface.

**PPMA (Polypropylene Magic)** — Proprietary build surface best suited for printing
Polypropylene (PP). Works similar to how PEI works for PLA. No glue needed and no release
agent needed. The most difficult prints stick and do not pull off. Flex plate post print
to remove parts. Can be cleaned with IPA. Heatbed is to be OFF the entire print. Set
220-230 °C for the hotend on the first layer; subsequent layers can be upwards of 270 °C
for the most durable prints. Surface is self-healing: if delamination occurs from the
spring steel, set plate upside down on the heatbed 30 minutes at 70-80 °C, cool before use.

**Carbon Fiber** — Similar to the G10 with SS plate except stiffer but still allowing
some degree of flex to release. Real authentic twill carbon fiber; thickness 0.5-1 mm.
NOT an effects plate — will not transfer a micro texture; very smooth surface. Works
great with PETG and PETG-CF. Works with ASA but will require Vision Miner's Nano Polymer
Adhesive and will self release clean once cool.
